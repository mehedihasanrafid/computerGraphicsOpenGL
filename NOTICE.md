## Original yacht design preservation

The `src/Yacht.cpp` module in this edition preserves the coordinate-based yacht artwork from the original collaborative repository, including the hull, decks, windows, upper structures, antenna, railings, and Bézier Bangladesh flag. The surrounding scenes and application infrastructure are a later portfolio reconstruction.
